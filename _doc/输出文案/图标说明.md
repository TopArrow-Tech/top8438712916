# LogScope 应用市场图标

- 文件：根目录 icon.png（发布包内置）；assets/logscope-icon.png（原设计文件）
- 规格：512×512 PNG，129843 字节，小于 200KB。
- 设计：深蓝底、日志文档、蓝色趋势线与青色折角，无文字。
- 生成方式：内置 imagegen；生成后缩小并优化 PNG 调色板，已人工查看成品。
- 用途：在应用市场发布表单中上传，所有版本共用；同时随安装包提供 icon.png，并通过 manifest.ui.icon 声明，供市场和客户端识别。

## 最终生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create one premium app marketplace icon for LogScope, a local Nginx log visualization tool. Square full-bleed opaque dark navy (#101a2b) background, no outside margin. Center a bold geometric document/log symbol with three short horizontal log lines, integrated with a clean rising analytics pulse line in vivid cornflower blue (#6495ff) and restrained cyan accent. Flat vector-like raster design, crisp thick strokes, generous breathing room, legible at 32px, refined developer-tool aesthetic matching a dark blue dashboard. No text, letters, numbers, watermark, brand logos, gradients, 3D effects, tiny details or mockup. The image itself is the icon, not a picture of an icon. Square PNG intended to resize to 512x512 and compress under 200KB.
